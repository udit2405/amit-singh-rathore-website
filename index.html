Meet Your Trainer

More Than 15 Years of Experience. One Focus: Your Progress.

Amit Singh Rathore has spent over fifteen years working as a fitness trainer, building a career around one central idea — that training only works when it's built around the person doing it.

Across more than a decade of coaching, Amit has worked with a wide range of clients, developing a particular focus on women's fitness — helping clients build strength, improve their physique, and develop training habits they can actually sustain.

Today, Amit works with clients internationally, including women across the USA and Australia, and is part of the trainer network at GroomFit, a premium fitness coaching brand. His approach is structured, personal, and rooted in the understanding that every body, lifestyle and goal is different.

Women's Fitness

Fitness Designed Around Women — Not Generic Workout Plans

Women's fitness shouldn't be approached as a collection of random exercises borrowed from a template. Your training should reflect where you're starting from — and where you want to go.

  • Individual fitness levels
  • Strength development
  • Body composition goals
  • Mobility
  • Training consistency
  • Lifestyle & work schedules
  • Recovery
  • Nutrition habits
  • Previous training experience
  • Personal goals

Whether your goal is getting stronger, improving your physique, losing body fat, feeling more confident, or simply building a sustainable fitness routine — your program should reflect who you are, not a generic template.

02
